# Acme Cloud — Billing & Policies

## Refund policy

Paid plans are billed monthly in advance. You may request a full refund within
**14 days** of your first payment, no questions asked. After 14 days, payments are
non-refundable, but you can cancel at any time to stop future charges. Refunds are
issued to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.

## Cancellation terms

You can cancel your subscription at any time from the billing page. Cancellation
takes effect at the end of the current billing period — you keep access until then
and are not charged again. There is no cancellation fee. Downgrading from Business
to Pro or Starter also takes effect at the end of the billing period.

## Service Level Agreement (SLA)

The Business plan includes a 99.9% monthly uptime SLA. If monthly uptime falls
below 99.9%, you are eligible for service credits: 10% of the monthly fee for
uptime between 99.0% and 99.9%, and 25% for uptime below 99.0%. Service credits
must be requested within 30 days of the affected month. The SLA does not apply to
the Starter or Pro plans.

## Data retention

Job logs are retained for 30 days on Starter, 90 days on Pro, and 1 year on Business.
After cancellation, your data is retained for 30 days and then permanently deleted.
You can export all data at any time via the API.
